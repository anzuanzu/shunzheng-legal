ShunZheng SOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

ShunZheng SOS (the "App") is an emergency evidence-recording tool. Our design principle is simple: your evidence belongs only to you. The App has no account system, and the developer operates no server that receives your video, audio, location, or contacts.

The App contains no advertising and no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. No data is used for advertising or shared with other companies.

2. iCloud backup

When iCloud backup is enabled, evidence is uploaded to the iCloud private database (CloudKit) tied to your Apple Account and protected by Apple's encryption and privacy safeguards. Only devices signed in to the same Apple Account can access it; the developer cannot. You can delete a single incident or all cloud data inside the App, or from the iCloud storage management screen in iOS Settings.

If you choose "self-hosted cloud" and enter your own server address, evidence is uploaded to that server. That server is operated by you or a third party of your choosing and is outside the scope of this policy.

3. Third-party apps

To notify guardians, the App opens Messages, LINE, WhatsApp, Telegram or the iOS share sheet with the message text and a map link pre-filled. Once you send the message, it is governed by that app and its terms. The App never sends a message before you tap Send.

4. Permissions

  • Camera and microphone: recording video and audio as evidence; in standby mode the microphone is used for keyword detection.
  • Speech recognition: detecting the rescue keyword, using on-device recognition whenever available.
  • Location (While Using / Always): recording your position during an incident; "Always" keeps updates flowing while the screen is locked.
  • Motion & Fitness: detecting the shake gesture.
  • Photos (add only): saving videos to your photo library.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: protecting the Records and Settings tabs, and verifying your identity when resetting a forgotten PIN.

Every permission can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings; the corresponding feature then stops working.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the App removes all local data; data in iCloud must be deleted separately inside the App or in iOS Settings. The developer cannot delete it on your behalf, because the developer never holds it.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect any personal data.

7. Legal notice

Audio and video recording is restricted by law in some countries and regions (for example, consent of the other party may be required). Please comply with local law when using the App. The App is not an emergency reporting tool and cannot replace your local emergency number.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its "last updated" date will be revised. Significant changes will be announced inside the App.
